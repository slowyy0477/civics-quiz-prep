# Civics & Community Engagement — Quiz Prep (HUM 208)

An offline, single-file study app for the HUM 208 (Civics & Community Engagement) quiz,
built from the two class decks: **Lecture 1 — Introduction to Civics and Community Engagement**
and **Active Citizenship P2 — Civic Engagement & Public Participation**.

## Use it

- **Online:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo.
- **Offline:** download `index.html` and double-click it — no internet, no dependencies, no install.
  On Windows, `Open study app.bat` opens it in your default browser.

## What's inside

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 · Exam map | What the two chapters actually test, plus the gaps worth asking a classmate about |
| 2 · Learn | Full lesson notes for both chapters (definitions, 5-item lists, Pakistan case studies) |
| 3 · Flashcards | 39 flip cards for active recall |
| 4 · Quiz | 44 multiple-choice questions with instant feedback, scoring, and a "revise these" list at the end |
| 5 · Written answers | 22 exam-style short-answer questions with model answers (answer out loud first, then reveal) |
| 6 · Cheat sheet | One-screen last-minute revision + a **Print / save as PDF** button |

## Coverage

**Chapter 1** — civics; citizenship (rights vs duties, birthright vs naturalised); importance of civic
and community engagement; the five key concepts (citizenship, democracy, rule of law, civic virtue,
public participation); active citizenship; civic engagement; community engagement; the Pakistan
example (Constitution 1973, voting age 18+, General Elections 2024); why a computing student studies civics.

**Chapter 2** — civil vs civics; forms of civic engagement; volunteering, advocacy, social activism;
case studies of public participation in Pakistan (Lawyers' Movement 2007-2009, 2005 earthquake relief,
youth-led education reform); the role of media; traditional vs social media; media literacy; media bias;
fake news and verification; citizen journalism; digital campaigns; challenges to media and solutions;
independent media in Pakistan; the future of media and civic engagement.

`CHEATSHEET.md` is the same cheat sheet in plain markdown for phone reading.
